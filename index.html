What is DISC?

DISC is a behavioral assessment tool that measures four primary personality types:

  • D - Dominance: Direct, results-oriented, decisive
  • I - Influence: Outgoing, enthusiastic, persuasive
  • S - Steadiness: Reliable, patient, supportive
  • C - Conscientiousness: Analytical, detail-oriented, accurate

How it works: This assessment contains 24 pairs of words. For each pair, select the word that is most like you and least like you. Your responses will be analyzed to determine your DISC profile.
